# Design log: Qwiic 6-DoF IMU breakout (rung1-zener-a1)

Authoring path: Zener (`pcb` 0.4.95) -> KiCad 10.0.1. Source of truth is `qwiic_imu.zen` + `parts/*.zen`.
Everything under `layout/qwiic_imu/` and `fab/` is generated by `tools/regen.sh`.

## 1. What the board is

ST LSM6DS3TR-C (LCSC C967633) on a 25.4 x 25.4 mm, 4-layer board. Two JST SH 1.0 mm Qwiic connectors
(J1 left edge, J2 right edge) wired in parallel, SA0 address jumper (JP1), 2.2 k pull-ups on SDA/SCL
(R1/R2) behind a cut-to-disable 3-pad jumper (JP2), unpopulated 0.1 in header (J3: INT1 INT2 SDA SCL),
100 nF at VDD and at VDDIO plus 10 uF bulk, two test points (3V3, GND), two M2.5 holes. No regulator, no LED.

## 2. Decisions and the numbers behind them

### Sensor wiring (datasheet Table 2 "Pin description", section 6.1 I2C)
| Pin | Name | Net | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SDO/SA0 | SA0 -> JP1 centre | address LSB; JP1 pad 1 (3V3) is bridged at the factory -> SA0 = 1 -> **0x6B**. Cut 1-2, bridge 2-3 -> **0x6A**. "If the SDO/SA0 pin is connected to the supply voltage, LSb is '1' (address 1101011b)". |
| 2, 3 | SDx, SCx | GND | sensor-hub interface unused: "Connect to VDDIO or GND". GND chosen (plane, no extra routing). |
| 4, 9 | INT1, INT2 | J3.1, J3.2 | push-pull outputs, brought to the header only; nothing else loads them. |
| 5 | VDDIO | 3V3 | single rail, VDDIO = VDD allowed (1.62 V .. VDD + 0.1 V). |
| 6, 7 | GND | GND | |
| 8 | VDD | 3V3 | 1.71 .. 3.6 V. |
| 10 (OCS), 11 (NC) | — | left open | "Leave unconnected". (KiCad symbol marks both no_connect; OCS is only used in mode 3.) |
| 12 | CS | 3V3 | I2C mode: "CS line must be tied high (i.e. connected to Vdd_IO)". Tied to the VDDIO rail. |
| 13, 14 | SCL, SDA | SCL, SDA | |

Datasheet provenance: `https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/lsm6ds3tr-c.pdf` served DocID026899 Rev 4
(LSM6DS3). The TR-C is the pin- and register-compatible successor (same LGA-14 2.5 x 3 x 0.83, same pin
table, same 0x6A/0x6B addresses); differences that matter here: WHO_AM_I is **0x6A** on the TR-C versus 0x69 on
the plain LSM6DS3, and typical current is lower (0.9 mA vs 1.25 mA in high-performance mode). The bring-up
guide uses this to detect a substituted part.

### Decoupling (datasheet section 7 "Application hints", Figure "electrical connections")
"Power supply decoupling capacitors (C1, C2 = 100 nF ceramic) should be placed as near as possible to the
supply pin of the device". ST's application figure adds 10 uF bulk on VDD.
- C1 100 nF (0603 X7R 50 V, C14663) at VDD pin 8: pad centre to pin centre **1.75 mm**, routed on F.Cu, 0.25 mm.
- C2 100 nF (same part) at VDDIO pin 5: **1.5 mm**, straight 0.2 mm trace between pins 5 and 6.
- C3 10 uF (0603 X5R 10 V, C96446) on VDD next to C1, 2.9 mm from pin 8, via to the 3V3 plane.
0603 instead of 0402 because the stock 0402 footprint silk sits 0.10 mm from the pads and the JLCPCB rule pack
wants 0.15 mm (a warning, but avoidable for free on a board this empty).

### I2C pull-ups: 2.2 k each
- Minimum (sink current): Rp >= (VDD - VOL) / IOL = (3.3 - 0.4) / 3 mA = **0.97 k**. 2.2 k sinks 1.5 mA per line.
- Maximum (rise time, Fast-mode 400 kHz, tr <= 300 ns): Rp <= tr / (0.8473 * Cb). With 2.2 k the bus may carry
  **Cb <= 161 pF** (the IMU's own pin is 5 pF; a Qwiic cable is ~50 pF/m; a 4-device chain with short cables
  stays well under this).
- Chains: every Qwiic board adds its pull-ups in parallel. Two boards -> 1.1 k (still legal), three -> 0.73 k
  (below the 0.97 k minimum). JP2 therefore lets the SDA link (pads 1-2) and the SCL link (pads 3-2) be cut on all
  but one board of a chain. Both links are closed by the factory bridge, so a single board works as shipped.

### Address choice
Default 0x6B (SA0 high). The host-side libraries (SparkFun LSM6DS3, Adafruit LSM6DS) default to 0x6B as
well; 0x6A is the alternate when two IMUs share a bus. JP1 is a 3-pad SPDT-style jumper so the alternate needs
one cut and one bridge, and the state is marked "6B" (bridged side) / "6A" on the silk.

### Power
3.3 V arrives only through the Qwiic connectors (and TP2 on the bench). Budget: 1.25 mA max for the IMU plus
2 x 1.5 mA while the bus lines are driven low -> under 5 mA, far below the Qwiic 3V3 rail. No LED (brief).

### Mechanical (brief)
- 25.4 x 25.4 mm, corner radius 2.0 mm, board origin at KiCad (100, 100).
- Holes H1/H2: 2.7 mm NPTH (M2.5, DIN965 courtyard) at (12.7, 2.54) and (12.7, 22.86) -> 20.32 mm apart on the
  vertical centre line. Connectors sit on the left/right edges, the two edges without holes.
- J1/J2 openings face outward; the housing face is 0.6 mm inside the edge, the retention tabs 0.43 mm from the
  edge (fab minimum 0.371 mm in Diode's JLCPCB PDK, 0.4 mm in the board's own rules).
- U1 at the board centre, pin 1 top-left.

### Axes
Datasheet Figure 1 (top view): the pin-1 dot sits on the corner where the 4-pad (long) side meets the 3-pad
(short) side; +X points along the short edge away from the dot, +Y along the long edge toward the dot, +Z out of
the top. With the KiCad footprint at rotation 0 (pin 1 top-left, pins 1-4 down the left side) that is **+X to
the right, +Y up, +Z out of the board**. Silk arrows X -> and Y ^ next to U1 show exactly that.

### Stackup and rules
JLCPCB stock 4-layer (stdlib `BASE_4L_STACKUP`: 1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner, 1.6 mm), HASL lead-free.
F.Cu: signals and a GND fill. In1.Cu: solid GND. In2.Cu: 3V3 pour. B.Cu: INT1/INT2, SA0 and one SCL stub,
GND fill. Design-margin rules written into the KiCad project from `qwiic_imu.zen`:
clearance 0.2 mm, track 0.25 mm (min 0.2), vias 0.6/0.3 mm, copper-to-edge 0.4 mm, hole-to-hole 0.5 mm,
silk text >= 1.0 mm / 0.15 mm. Pours are inset 0.5 mm from the edge. The JLCPCB rule pack
(`layout/qwiic_imu/qwiic_imu.kicad_dru`, copied from kicad-druid) is the fab backstop.

### Routing
All I2C on F.Cu: SDA runs at y=109.5 straight into J1 pin 3 and J2 pin 3 (after one jog), SCL at y=110.2 into
J2 pin 4 and, through a via pair on B.Cu, into J1 pin 4. The header taps (J3 pins 3/4) drop straight down from
the buses. INT1/INT2 cross under the IC on B.Cu. 25 vias total; every plane connection is a dedicated via.

## 3. Tool-chain decisions (and workarounds)
- **Part fields.** Zener puts `Mpn / Manufacturer / Lcsc / Datasheet` on the PCB footprints but exports no
  custom fields onto schematic symbol instances, and a `properties=` override on a stdlib generic is dropped.
  So `parts/Resistor.zen`, `parts/Capacitor.zen` are thin `Component()` wrappers with an `lcsc` config, and
  `tools/sync_schematic.py` copies the footprint fields onto the symbol instances as `MPN / Manufacturer /
  LCSC / Datasheet / Package` after every `pcb apply schematic`. `tools/layout_board.py` renames the footprint
  fields `Lcsc -> LCSC`, `Mpn -> MPN` so KiCad's parity check sees identical names on both sides.
- **Symbols/footprints** not in the Diode stdlib (Sensor_Motion LSM6DS3, JST SH SM04B, LGA-14, Bridged123
  jumper, TestPoint, Conn_01x04_MountingPin) are extracted verbatim from the KiCad 10 stock libraries
  into `parts/symbols` and `parts/footprints` (`tools/extract_symbol.py`).
- **ERC power source.** 3V3 and GND enter through passive connector pins. The canonical fix (PWR_FLAG) does
  not survive Zener: every `Component()` needs a footprint, and the schematic exporter aborts on a PWR_FLAG
  symbol ("visible terminal belongs to 0 physical schematic islands"). The two rail test points therefore use a
  symbol variant whose single pin is `power_out` (`parts/symbols/TestPoint_PowerSource.kicad_sym`), which is
  also where a bench supply is injected during bring-up.
- **Net labels.** The exporter writes local labels, which KiCad names `/SDA` while the board nets are `SDA`;
  the sync script converts them to global labels on this single sheet. The two remaining parity warnings are
  KiCad's `unconnected-(U1-NC-Pad10)` vs Zener's `unconnected-(U1.U:10)` naming of the no-connect pads.
- **Library warnings.** The generated schematic uses bare `lib_id`s; `tools/make_symlib.py` builds a project
  symbol library from the schematic's own `lib_symbols` block and a `sym-lib-table` with an empty nickname.
  Without it ERC reports 32 `lib_symbol_issues` warnings.
- **Layout from code.** `tools/layout_board.py` (pcbnew, headless) owns placement, routing, planes and silk.
  `pcb layout --no-open` preserves footprint positions and tracks on re-sync; the script deletes and rebuilds
  its own items, so a regeneration from `.zen` is one `tools/regen.sh` run. KiCad 10 SWIG notes: use
  `board.Delete()` not `Remove()` (Remove leaks the item and corrupts later calls), do not call
  `GetNetsByName()` (same effect), use `board.FindNet()`.
- Jumper footprints are the stock "Bridged" ones (copper bridge polygons). Diode's DFM cannot attribute that
  copper to a net and marks its copper-clearance rule "incomplete"; KiCad DRC checks the same geometry (0 errors).

### Every warning, with a reason
- ERC `pin_to_pin`: U1 pin 2 (SDx, bidirectional) and TP1 pin 1 (power output) share GND. SDx is tied to GND
  as the datasheet instructs; the "power output" is the ERC-source test point described above. Harmless.
- DRC parity `net_conflict` x2: no-connect pads 10/11 named differently by KiCad and Zener. No copper involved.
- `pcb build`: "Pin '1' on component 'TP' is a power pin but is connected to plain Net" x2 -- Zener's lint
  does not see through the module boundary; the nets are `Power("3V3")` and `Ground("GND")`.
- `kicad-cli sch export netlist`: "schematic has annotation errors" -- the exporter gives power symbols
  hash-suffixed references (`#PWR0622ff...`); the netlist is complete (38/38 checks).
- `pcb dfm` verdict "fail" is driven only by the "incomplete" copper-clearance rule (0 findings, 0 failed rules).

## 5. Open issues
- LCSC stock for C967633 was not confirmed live (no API; the LCSC product page loaded, the datasheet did not).
  JLCPCB may offer the plain LSM6DS3 as a substitute; the WHO_AM_I step in BRINGUP.md catches that.
- The TR-C-specific datasheet PDF could not be downloaded (ST timed out / served the LSM6DS3 document, Mouser
  returned a bot page). All pin, address and decoupling facts above are from DocID026899 Rev 4 and are the same
  for the TR-C; current figures were not re-verified.
- Connector face is 0.6 mm inside the board edge (SparkFun boards are flush). Cables mate fine with SH housings
  at that inset, but a flush variant would need the retention tabs to move inside the 0.371 mm copper-to-edge
  limit or a different footprint.
- No Zener `TestBench` tests (`pcb test` finds none); the netlist is verified by `tools/verify_netlist.py` on the
  exported KiCad netlist instead.
- The 3D model shown in the renders for U1 is the generic LGA body; no STEP was embedded.
