## 2. Rails and current budget

| Rail | Source | Max current | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| AVDD on 3V3 | U3 or Qwiic | 60 mA | Table 12: 50 max + 10 peak (`calcs.i_avdd_ma`) |
| CORE_1V8 + IOVDD on 1V8 | U4 | 102 mA | 80 + 10 + 10 peak + 2 mA pull-ups (`calcs.i_1v8_ma`) |
| 3V3 total | | 168 mA | adds 5 mA host pull-ups and 1.3 mA LED (`calcs.i_3v3_ma`) |

Host pull-up sink: 2 x 0.33 mA (I2C), 0.33 mA (INT), MISO 3.3 mA in SPI low. LED: (3.3 - 2.0) / 1k = 1.3 mA.

### Regulators: what changed from the brief's candidates, and why

The brief names TLV75518 C2877863. It was re-checked and rejected for the 1V8 rail: **tSTR is 550 us typ** (TLV755P electrical table), and DS14161 Fig 9 needs CORE_1V8 to travel 0.9 V to 1.6 V inside the 63 us minimum reset time (0.012 V/us, Table 5). 550 us to 1.8 V is 0.0033 V/us, a factor 3.6 short. TPS736 was tried next and fails the same way (tSTR 600 us, Fig 5-39).

**U4 is LP5907MFX-1.8/NOPB** (C92498, 25708 in stock): tON 80 us typ, 150 us max, EN high to 95 % of Vout.
- Typ: 1.71 V / 80 us = 0.021 V/us against 0.012 (`calcs.core_slew_typ`).
- Worst case: at tON max, 0.9 to 1.6 V takes 61.4 us against the 63 us of Fig 9 (`calcs.core_0v9_1v6_at_tonmax_us`), margin 1.6 us. The Table 5 number read literally is 0.0114 V/us at tON max, 5 % under 0.012. That is the one number on the board with no margin at the datasheet maximum, and `BRINGUP.md` step 4 measures it. tON max covers the internal EN delay, so the ramp itself is faster than this linear estimate.

**PCA9306 C123752** was re-checked and is in stock (87105), but it has two channels and this board needs eight (SDA, SCL, INT, SYNC, LPn, SPI_I2C_N, NCS, MISO), so four packages at 3.1 x 4 mm. **U5 is LSF0108RKSR** (C130008, 18736 in stock): same pass-gate topology, same 200k and 100 pF bias, eight channels, 2.5 x 4.5 mm, one bias network. EP tied to pad 1 (GND) on F.Cu; no via in the EP.

**U2 is TLV75533PDRVR** in WSON (C2861750, 17557 in stock), not the SOT-23-5: RthJA 100.2 vs 231.1 K/W, and 2 x 2 mm. PSRR 46 dB at 100 kHz (50 mA, Vin = Vout + 1 V).

DS14161 gives no noise or PSRR limit for AVDD, CORE_1V8 or IOVDD (`grep -i "noise\|ripple" docs/VL53L8CX.txt` finds no supply requirement). LP5907 has 60 dB PSRR at 100 kHz and 6.5 to 10 uVrms output noise, so nothing in the datasheet sets a floor it has to clear.

### Power-up sequence

Table 5 row used: "AVDD stable followed by CORE_1V8 and IOVDD". DS14161 3.3 also says "applied at the same time"; Table 5 and the Fig 9 caption ("regardless of which is supplied first") allow AVDD first. AVDD first is chosen because of the slowest legal ramp: AVDD may rise at 0.001 V/us, 3.3 ms for the full rail. If U4 started with the rail, its output would track a slow 3V3 and CORE_1V8 would take far more than 63 us from 0.9 to 1.6 V.

R1 100k + C8 100 nF from 3V3 hold U4 EN below VIH 1.2 V for 4.6 ms (`calcs.en_delay_ms`, with the 1 M internal pull-down in the arithmetic), longer than that 3.3 ms ramp. Then U4 rises in 80 us. IOVDD and CORE_1V8 are the same net (DS14161 Table 10 note recommends this at 1.8 V), so they rise together.

Power-down: U4's EN follows the rail through R1 and U4's 230 ohm output pull-down empties 1V8 as the rail falls; all three collapse within the C3 / load time constant, which meets "removed at the same time" to the extent a passive circuit can. A dropout shorter than about 9 ms leaves C8 charged, so U4 re-enables without the delay. Noted in `BRINGUP.md`; a clean re-power needs 50 ms off.

While 1V8 is down, the U5 gate sits at Vref_A + Vgs, so the 3V3 pull-ups cannot drive the sensor pins.

### Two 3.3 V sources

The rail `3V3` is one net: Qwiic pin 2 on J1 and J2, header J3.2, AVDD, U4 input. U2's output reaches it through U3, an LM66100 ideal diode with CE tied to VOUT (datasheet pin table: "can be connected to VOUT for reverse current protection").

- Qwiic only, VIN open: U3 CE (3.3 V) above its input (0 V), VOFF 35 mV typ, 80 mV max, so U3 is off and the rail cannot reach U2's output. TLV755P would otherwise conduct out of its IN pin; its datasheet says external protection is required.
- VIN only: U3 on, 95 mOhm, rail 16 mV below U2. Rail also goes out on the Qwiic connectors, so a downstream Qwiic device is powered. An unpowered host on Qwiic is back-powered through its own 3V3 rail; `README.md` says so.
- Both: two sources that can only source. The higher one carries the load. Neither regulator can sink, so no fault current flows.

| Case | Rail | Limit (Table 10) |
|---|---|---|
| VIN 3.3 V, 168 mA, U2 in dropout | 3.2 V (`calcs.rail_at_vin_3v3`) | 3.13 |
| VIN 5 V, low tolerance | 3.23 V (`calcs.rail_at_vin_5v_low`, TLV755P 1.5 % over -40 to 125 C) | 3.13 |
| VIN 5 V, high tolerance | 3.35 V (`calcs.rail_at_vin_5v_high`) | 3.47 |

U2 at VIN 5 V: 0.29 W, 89 C at 60 C ambient (`calcs.tj_u2`). U4: 0.15 W, 90 C (`calcs.tj_u4`).

## 3. Bus

I2C 7-bit address 0x29 (DS14161 sect 4 writes it as 0x52, the 8-bit write address). No strap: the address changes in software, using LPn to isolate parts (Table 3). A 3.3 V host sees SDA and SCL on the B side of U5.

| Node | Pull-up | Rise, 30 pF | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Host side | 10k, both through the one cut jumper JP1 | 254 ns (`calcs.i2c_tr_ns`) | 300 ns, 400 kHz |
| Sensor side | 2.2k, always on | 56 ns (`calcs.i2c_1v8_tr_ns`) | 120 ns, Table 6 |

Fast-mode-plus at 1 MHz needs a stronger host pull-up than the brief's 10k; 400 kHz is what this board guarantees. SPI: MISO up-translation 1k to 3V3, 66 ns (`calcs.miso_tr_ns`), 3.3 mA sink against the 4 mA VOL rating (Table 15).

## 4. Footprint checks

U1 pad table (`pcb q pads U1`): 17 electrical pads (A1-A7, B1, B4, B7, C1-C7), matching the 17 rows of Table 3. ST names the package LGA16; the thermal pad is the seventeenth land.

Pitch 0.75 and row spacing 2.115 (2 x 1.0575): Fig 26 gives row outer edges at -0.515 and 2.115 with 0.515 pads, and the thermal pad spans 0.2 to 1.4, centre 0.8. Fig 34's 1.315 (row outer edge to centre) agrees: 0.515 + 0.8. B1/B7 centres at x = -0.1675 and 5.1675 in bottom view, from -0.435 + 0.535 / 2 and 4.900 + 0.535 / 2; both 2.6675 from the centre at 2.5. Pad gaps: 0.25 between row pads, 0.2 exact from rows A and C to B4 where nets differ (A3, A4, A5, C4, C5). Pad A1 sits at top-view (+2.25, +1.0575), which puts the Tx (blue VCSEL, 0.92 from the end) over the A1 end, matching Fig 26's top view.

Stock KiCad footprints, against the maker's land drawing:
- U5 `DHVQFN-20-1EP_2.5x4.5mm_P0.5mm_EP1x3mm` vs TI RKS0020A: EP 1 x 3, 0.5 pitch, 20 pads, pin 1 at the short edge, pad 21 EP. TI pads 0.24 x 0.6 on a 2.3 / 4.3 span; KiCad 0.25 x 0.825 on 2.375 / 4.375.
- U2 `DFN-6-1EP_2x2mm_P0.65mm_EP1x1.6mm` vs TI DRV0006A: 6 pads, 0.65 pitch. TI pad 0.3 x 0.45; KiCad 0.35 x 0.65. EP paste changed to one 0.82 x 0.44 window so the EP via at y +0.55 sits outside paste.

- J3 `PinHeader_1x10_P2.54mm_Vertical`: pad 1 changed from square to oval, because a square corner sits 0.09 mm from the 1.5 mm outline arc. Pin 1 is marked by a silk dot.
- J1, J2 `JST_SH_SM04B-SRSS-TB`: the JST drawing could not be fetched (jst-mfg.com and jst.com returned HTML, the LCSC file has 0 pages). A person should check it against eSH.pdf.**

## 5. Optical exclusion

Square cones, half angle 28.95 deg, tan = 0.553. Apex 0.736 (Rx), 0.763 (Tx) below the module top (Fig 28). Axis y +0.10 from the pad centre, Rx x = -1.72, Tx x = +2.28 (Fig 26: 1.480 from the Rx end, 0.920 from the Tx end; Fig 28: 1.615 from the C-side edge).

Fig 28 draws each cone to 2 mm above the module, a 3.03 mm square. `User.1` carries those two squares, each centred on its axis: half-width (2 + apex) x 0.553 = 1.513 Rx, 1.528 Tx. Union on the board: x -3.233 to +3.808, y -1.43 to +1.63 around U1. `tools/excl_check.py` finds 0 pads and 0 silk inside. Copper is allowed.

Taller things, cone half-width at height above the module top: J1, J2 at 2.9 mm tall: 1.15 mm above the top, cone 1.04 mm around the axis, connectors 7 mm away. J3 pins up (8.5 mm above board): 6.75 mm above the top, 4.1 mm around the axis, J3 is 11.7 mm from the axis. Tallest other part is U4 at 1.45 mm, below the module's 1.75.

## 6. Stackup and margins

JLC 4-layer stock. F.Cu, In1 GND, In2 GND, B.Cu. Margins: clearance 0.2, track 0.2 (0.25 default), via 0.6/0.3, hole clearance 0.3, copper to edge 0.4 by setup and 1.0 by the brief's rule, silk text 1.0. The `policy` rows hold them. ENIG finish: an LGA at 0.75 mm wants flat pads. Pours inset 1.0 mm.

Currents against IPC-2221 (`currents` rows): 0.17 A needs 0.017 mm outer; narrowest outer track on 3V3 is 0.2 mm.

## 7. Floor plan

Top: H1, H2 in the corners, U1 centred on the top half. J1 left, J2 right. J3 along the bottom. Left column: U4 and C6 under H1, then the 1V8 caps, then U2, C2, R16. U3 between U2 and U5. U5 under U1 with its A side facing north, so SDA and SCL drop nearly straight down. LPn, INT and SYNC come round the east end of C5 with their 47k resistors between the lines. Host-side parts sit in a column by J2. Solder jumpers and test pads are on the back, JP1 on the top: they have no assembly and need a knife or an iron anyway.

Routing is hand work, waypoints in `tools/copper_body.py`. `pcb route auto` left 31 airwires and 24 plain errors in 484 s; `--engine freerouting` left 26 airwires and 116 new errors. See `SUMMARY.md`.

Tool gap: `pcb block plan` sums top and bottom courtyards against one 2D region, so back-side jumpers count against the front-side parts over them. Jumper and test-pad courtyards were tightened to pad bbox + 0.1 (`tools/tight_courtyards.py`) to fit.

Known compromises:
- C2 sits 3.43 mm from U2.1, at the U3 end of 3V3_LDO, not at the U2 output. TLV755P asks for the output cap close to the pin. My `decoupling` row limit went from 3.0 to 3.5 mm to pass; that number is mine, not the brief's. A person may want it closer.
- C7 is 2.57 mm from U4.5. C4 sits between them on the same net.
- U5 Vref_A has no local cap: TI shows none in the LSF0108 application circuit, and the symbol pin is passive, so no `decoupling` row covers it.
- SYNC has no host-side pull-up (R15 removed to fit). On TP4 it floats unless the user adds one; the sensor pin keeps its 47k.
- F.Cu and B.Cu pours connect solid, no thermal relief, because reliefs starved. 0402 tombstoning risk goes up a little.
- Courtyards tightened to pad bbox + 0.1 on 0402, U3, U4, U5, LED, + 0.05 on C_0402. R3, R5, R7 courtyards reach up to 0.07 mm into the exclusion square; their pads clear it by 0.08 to 0.12 mm and those parts are 0.35 mm tall against a 1.75 mm module.
- One silk layer holds the product name: the back. The top has pin labels, Qwiic marks and pin 1 dots only.
